How it works

From scope agreement
to signed change order.

The entire workflow lives in your inbox. No jumping between apps, no copy-pasting into dashboards.

01   Before the project

Define your scope

Set deliverables, rate, timeline and payment terms. Send your client a scope agreement to sign digitally. Both parties receive a signed copy by email automatically.

02   During the project

Catch it in the email

When a client emails asking for something extra, click Analyze. Crept reads that email and checks it against your signed scope with AI — giving you a RED, YELLOW, or GREEN verdict in seconds.

03   Get paid

Send the change order

Build a change order with line items and pricing in seconds. Your client approves with one click from their inbox — no account needed. Both parties get a signed copy. No awkward conversations. No free work.

What do the verdicts mean?

RED
Scope creep

The request is clearly outside your signed scope. Send a change order before doing any work.

YELLOW
Borderline

The request is ambiguous — it might be covered, it might not. Worth a conversation before proceeding.

GREEN
In scope

The request is covered by your signed scope. Carry on — no action needed.
