2. What Crept is

Crept is a Chrome extension that lives inside Gmail and helps freelancers detect scope creep in client emails. It does this by comparing email content against a signed scope agreement using AI, and returns a verdict along with a draft change order.

Crept is a tool to assist your judgement — not a substitute for professional legal or financial advice. AI analysis results are indicative and should be reviewed before acting on them.
